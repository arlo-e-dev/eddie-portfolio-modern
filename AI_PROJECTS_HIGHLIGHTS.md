## Why Agentic AI?

Traditional AI applications are **reactive** - they respond to user prompts. 

**Agentic AI is proactive** - agents can:
- Make autonomous decisions
- Use tools and APIs
- Collaborate with other agents
- Maintain long-term goals
- Adapt to changing environments
- Learn from feedback

This shift represents the future of AI applications - moving from chatbots to intelligent assistants that can reason, plan, and execute complex workflows with minimal human intervention.
